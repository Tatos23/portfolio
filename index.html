About

I work at the intersection of applied machine learning and systems engineering: building models that run against real sensors, real constraints, and real latency budgets rather than static benchmarks. My thesis at the Human-Centered AI lab, Universität Augsburg (Prof. Elisabeth André), produced a real-time wearable stress-detection and intervention system, completed with grade A+.

I am a Category "A" scholar at the GUC and a national-level competitive swimmer, which is where I learned that consistent, measured training beats sporadic intensity — in the pool and in research.

Publications

EAI PervasiveHealth 2025 · Springer LNICST · in press

Design of Wearable-Based Real-Time Stress Detection and Breathing Intervention for Workplace Well-Being

Abdelrahman Ewida, Thomas Kiderle, Elisabeth André, Bhargavi Mahesh

Lead author. End-to-end system that streams ECG from a Polar H10 chest sensor over BLE, extracts HRV features in sliding windows, classifies stress with a Random Forest, and delivers a context-aware guided breathing intervention that defers to the user (no interruption while typing; user confirms before it starts).

81.05% mean accuracy under leave-one-subject-out cross-validation on WESAD (15 subjects), deployed in a live React Native / Flask pipeline at ~130 Hz.

ICEENG-IPCS 2026 · IEEE · in press

Optimization of Multi-AGV Scheduling for Airport Baggage Handling

Abdallah A. Hassan, Abdelrahman Ewida, Haidy E. Elkenawy, Abdulrahman B. Bahy, Karim M. Fathy, Dalia M. Mahfouz, Omar M. Shehata

Co-author. Multi-objective VRPTW formulation for airport baggage AGVs, benchmarking Simulated Annealing, Genetic Algorithm, Ant Colony Optimization, and Q-Learning against a proposed Hybrid Artificial Bee Colony algorithm combining discrete routing operators with continuous start-time perturbation.

Hybrid ABC improved operational efficiency by ~61% over the FCFS baseline while cutting fleet size from 11 to 3 AGVs on the standard instance; Q-Learning traded optimality for ~1 s execution, motivating a hierarchical plan-then-recover architecture.
